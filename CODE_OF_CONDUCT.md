# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

Everyone participating in Samsarix Core is expected to help create a welcoming,
safe, and respectful environment regardless of age, body size, disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity or expression, experience,
education, socioeconomic status, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or
sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, patient, and accountable.
- Welcome different backgrounds and viewpoints.
- Focus criticism on ideas and code, not people.
- Respect privacy and obtain consent before sharing personal information.
- Accept reasonable moderation decisions and disengage when asked.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, sexualized attention, threats, intimidation, doxxing,
stalking, sustained disruption, abuse of privileged access, and retaliation against
someone who reports a concern are not acceptable.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and
other spaces that the repository owner designates as official. It also applies when
someone officially represents the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to `support@samsarix.com`. Do not include personal
or sensitive details in a public issue. Samsarix LLC is responsible for investigating
reports, protecting reporters' privacy as far as practical, resolving conflicts of
interest, and choosing a proportionate response such as correction, warning,
temporary restriction, or permanent removal.

This repository does not currently promise a particular response time or separate
appeal committee. Appeals can be sent to the same private address and should state
that they concern a moderation decision.

## Attribution

This policy is informed by the Contributor Covenant and established Python and
Django community codes of conduct, but the text above is the policy that applies
to this repository.
